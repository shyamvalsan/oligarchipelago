# Press contacts research: The Oligarchipelago

Research date: 2026-10-29. Nothing has been sent and nobody has been contacted. This is a research list for the maintainer to act on personally.

## Method and limits

- Every contact route below was fetched in this research session from the outlet's own page. Routes were not guessed from patterns, taken from directories, or scraped.
- Phone numbers and private emails are deliberately omitted. Where an outlet's page shows a corporate editorial address, it is quoted as the outlet publishes it.
- "Beat: inferred from byline page" means a fetched author page or bio says so. It is not a claim that the person wants to receive this pitch.
- Web search quota ran out partway through the session. Later relevance checks relied on direct fetches of outlet topic and tag pages. Some outlets could not be checked that way and are listed under "Unverified".
- Rule applied: where a page blocked access (403, 401, Cloudflare challenge, JavaScript-only), the outlet is in "Unverified", not in the contact tables.

## E. Etiquette note

- Personalise every message. Name the specific recent article that made you pick that reporter, and say what the data adds to it.
- No bulk email. Send one pitch at a time to one desk, and do not copy several outlets on the same message.
- Disclose up front that the project is AI-assisted, not independently reviewed, and a research preview. It is not a legal finding.
- Offer the data and the methodology: the rubric (political leverage, preferential economic benefit, wealth-power feedback), the sources behind each assessment, and the row-level data.
- Make no embargo promises you cannot keep. If you offer an embargo, put the end time in writing. Note that Light Reading's page asks reporters to be asked first.
- Give a corrections contact and a place to dispute a rating. Say how quickly you will fix errors.
- Do not use "oligarch" as a conclusion about any named person. The project uses a rubric, and individual ratings are claims that need a source.
- Do not use the confidential tip lines (Bloomberg, Guardian, Rest of World, MediaNama's anonymous form) for pitches. They are for tips, and Bloomberg says so explicitly.
- Respect each outlet's stated use of its address. Several above (BS, Mint, Indian Express, HT) are feedback or editorial-query addresses, not pitch desks.
