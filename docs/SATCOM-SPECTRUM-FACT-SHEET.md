# Satellite-spectrum policy in India: dated fact sheet (Starlink, Jio, Airtel)

Compiled 2026-10-29 for journalists to check. It records what documents say; it does not adjudicate Elon Musk's October 2026 allegation or the government's reply. Every line carries a URL that was opened during compilation.

**Source labels.** [P] primary: a government, regulator, court or company document read in full or in the passages cited. [S] secondary: press or analysis; reported figures and quotations were not checked against the underlying document unless stated. Where a primary document could not be opened, the line says so.

## 2. What each party has said (as documented)

- **Reliance Jio / Mukesh Ambani.** Filed positions in 2023, 2024 and 2026 are consistent: auction, "same service, same rules", level playing field with terrestrial operators (links above). Jio holds a satellite licence through Jio Satellite Communications, has a joint venture with SES, announced a SpaceX distribution agreement in March 2025 and a plan for its own constellation in 2026. Filings were signed by company officers, not by Mukesh Ambani. I found no Reliance or Ambani statement answering Musk (CNBC said it had asked; https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/08/elon-musk-starlink-spacex-jio-airtel-india.html).
- **Airtel / Sunil Mittal.** 2023: auctioning is "neither reasonable nor fair". 2024: level playing field for urban retail; differential pricing for traditional uses. March 2025: Starlink distribution agreement. 2026: apply the 2025 framework to new network operators (links above). Bharti Space is a major shareholder in Eutelsat and Mittal is its co-chairman (https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/sunil-mittals-bharti-group-joins-french-govt-to-raise-155-bn-for-starlink-rival-eutelsat), so Airtel's group has an interest in OneWeb, one of the three licensed operators. I found no Airtel or Mittal statement answering Musk.
- **Starlink / SpaceX.** Says it has built 20 gateway sites and operations for India and "has spent five years complying" (https://www.medianama.com/2026/10/223-elon-musk-monopolisitc-oligarchs-starlink/). In 2024 comments Starlink pressed for a single rule for all operators and a low spectrum charge (reported in https://www.medianama.com/2024/11/223-starlink-and-telcos-debate-level-playing-field-between-telecom-and-satcom-in-trai-consultation/; the Starlink 2024 comment PDF is mostly image-only and was not read).
- **Government.** Administrative assignment is lawful under the Act and global practice (Rajya Sabha reply above); pricing is TRAI's; security clearance is the Home Ministry's; all three licensees are at the same stage (PIB, 8 October 2026).
- **TRAI.** Sees no near-term competitive substitution that would raise a level-playing-field issue (para 2.39 of the 2025 recommendations); levies the same 4% AGR on every NGSO operator.

## 3. What is disputed

1. **Whether satellite broadband falls within First Schedule entry 16.** TRAI's reading is yes (2025 paper, para 2.25). Some stakeholders argue entry 16 covers only "certain" services and, for GMPCS, mobile service only, and that each entry must satisfy Section 4(5)(a) (same paper, paras 2.4–2.5; stakeholders are not named there). Jio's 2024 and 2026 filings press the legal-risk argument.
2. **Whether the Supreme Court's 2G rulings require auction for satellite spectrum.** Jio says yes; Airtel's 2023 counter-comments say the judgment "didn't mandate auction as the sole method in every case". I did not open the judgments.
3. **Whether satellite and terrestrial services compete.** Jio, Airtel and COAI say increasingly yes; TRAI says not in the near-to-medium term.
4. **Whether Starlink is being treated unequally.** Musk says it is blocked; the government says all three licensees are at broadly the same stage. PIB's September 2025 backgrounder says Jio Satellite Communication and OneWeb India were licensed before Starlink received its licence in June 2025 (https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2170091). I found no document comparing the three operators' security files, so equal treatment can be neither confirmed nor refuted from public documents.
5. **Whether any individual is influencing the outcome.** Musk's post does not name anyone; his Oct 8 post names Ambani (verified above); no document I read shows any person's involvement in the decisions. The positions of Jio and Airtel in TRAI filings are public corporate lobbying, which is distinct from influence over the outcome, and the outcome so far (administrative assignment, a uniform 4% AGR charge) differs from Jio's stated preference.

## 4. What is not known

- Whether the Home Ministry has cleared any of the three operators, and on what timetable.
- The final spectrum price, whether Cabinet approves 4% or a higher rate, and when assignment will occur.
- Whether the June 2026 draft rules will be extended to NGSO broadband.
- Whether Jio, Reliance, Airtel or Mittal has responded to Musk's posts.
- The exact timing and wording of Musk's follow-up post, which I have only through MediaNama and The Next Web.
- The content of the Jio and SpaceX March 2025 exchange filing, the Bloomberg report on frozen approvals, the Economic Times report on the DCC decision, and the text of the 2G Supreme Court judgments. Each is cited only through secondary reports or not at all.
- Whether TRAI-hosted comments on the 2026 paper from other operators show a different alignment; I read only Jio, Airtel and Starlink.
- Any evidence about the basis for Musk's allegation.
