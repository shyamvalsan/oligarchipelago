# Data and editorial model — 2026-10-08

## Universe and units

- Archive: 500 Forbes annual 2026 entries, preserving tied ranks and family records. Launch app: the first 200 entries in that saved order, selected by `lib/universe.mjs`, not by assessment availability. Wealth date: 2026-03-01. Wealth is billions of USD, not live prices.
- 2024 World Bank GDP, GDP/person, GDP PPP and PPP/person retain observation years and URLs. Country is Forbes citizenship, not residence or location of every asset.
- Launch: 199 GDP matches and one unmatched entry (Terry Gou, Taiwan). Archive: 492 matches and eight unmatched Taiwanese entries. No proxy or imputed zero.
- Economic footprint = wealth × 1e9 / annual GDP × 100. This is a stock-to-flow comparison, not GDP owned or political influence. PPP uses international dollars and is not divided into USD wealth.
- Forbes self-made flag retained. Non-self-made scores 1–2: inherited; 3–5: inherited-and-grown. Missing detail: family-linked, unspecified. Origin never adds assessment points.

## Active spectrum ledger

`assessments.json` is generated by `scripts/build-spectrum.py` from authored cases and discovery records. `scripts/build-assessments.py` produces `cases.json`, merging `additional-cases.json`, authored in `scripts/extend-cases.py`. The retired `evidence.json` pilot is not loaded at runtime. Legacy status fields remain for provenance, not displayed spectrum categories.

Operational definition: private wealth and political power materially reinforce one another through preferential access, policy influence, state patronage or an exchange securing private economic advantage. Oligarchy is not synonymous with a crime.

### Published scoring rule

Each dimension is an authored integer from 0 to 4:

- Political leverage: 25%.
- Preferential economic benefit: 25%.
- Wealth–power feedback: 50%.

`score = round(25 × (0.25 × leverage + 0.25 × benefit + 0.50 × feedback))`

Anchors: 0 = reviewed evidence against the mechanism within scope; 1 = contextual or weak connection; 2 = specific but contested or incomplete link; 3 = substantial institutional, scholarly or corroborated support; 4 = unusually strong direct or adjudicated support for that dimension. These are editorial anchors, not objectively comparable measurements across countries.

A missing dimension is null and prevents a score. Unknown never means zero, midpoint or ordinary. Every estimate exposes dimension rationales, receipt references, counterevidence, qualitative confidence, historical period, limitations and a plausible editorial range. Ranges are not statistical intervals; scores are not probabilities of guilt. Small differences should not be treated as meaningful ranks. No automatic points for nationality, inheritance, sanctions, donations, contracts or company size.

These initial AI-assisted judgments have not received independent editorial review. Coverage and source selection are uneven. The 147 launch-cohort reviews without estimates (156 in the archive) are not exonerations. Waves 21–35 were written under a shared search budget that ran out partway: many receipts are Wikipedia or fetch-tool summaries (marked in each receipt's access field), and several concern a relative or company rather than the named person. Treat them as starting points for verification.

## Chart semantics

The dividers exactly bisect the plotting rectangle on desktop and mobile. Default wealth is logarithmic from $2.5B to $1T, with $50B at the midpoint. Linear mode is $0–$1T, changing the divider to $500B. The spectrum runs 0–100, midpoint 50. Quadrants are Billionaires, Mega-Billionaires, Oligarchs and Mega-Oligarchs: regional labels, not four definitive verdicts.

The background shades continuously from Just another billionaire to Oligarch. Markers default to local citizenship flags; continent and origin colors are optional. Citizenship does not assign political allegiance. Exact coordinates use no jitter; overlap access, search and table provide individual access. Unplaced entries are listed separately, never positioned arbitrarily. The selected scored person's editorial range appears on the chart.

Economic mode uses a logarithmic 0.001%–1,000% GDP axis centered on 1%. CSV exports scores, ranges, confidence, period, review date and limitations, with null scores blank.

## Maintenance and launch limits

Rebuild authored files deliberately, in order:

```sh
python3 scripts/extend-cases.py
python3 scripts/build-assessments.py
python3 scripts/build-spectrum.py
node --test tests/*.test.mjs
```

Builders reproduce authored judgments; they do not independently validate sources. Case changes require identity checks, source passages, legal updates, alternative explanations and responses. Independent editorial review, a corrections contact, remaining individual research and Forbes source-reuse review are outstanding. Public availability is not a republication license. This repository is a development checkpoint, not approval to launch the website.
